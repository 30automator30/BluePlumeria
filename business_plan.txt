================================================================
1. BUSINESS OVERVIEW
================================================================

Blue Plumeria Jewelry is a handcrafted jewelry studio specializing
in beaded necklaces, bracelets, and earrings. Each piece is
designed and assembled by hand using high-quality materials
including Miyuki and Toho seed beads, Preciosa and Swarovski
crystals, Czech glass, natural gemstones, and precious metal
findings.

The business operates from a dedicated home studio in Bastrop, TX
and sells through online channels and local markets.

Business structure: Sole Proprietorship
NAICS code: 339910 (Jewelry Manufacturing)
Year established: 2025

================================================================
2. PRODUCTS
================================================================

Product lines:
  - Beaded statement necklaces ($45-$150)
  - Gemstone and crystal bracelets ($25-$75)
  - Handcrafted earrings ($15-$45)
  - Custom/commission pieces (price varies)

Materials used:
  - Japanese seed beads (Miyuki, Toho) in sizes 11/0 and 15/0
  - Czech glass beads (fire-polished, SuperDuo, StormDuo, DropDuo)
  - Preciosa rivoli crystals
  - Natural stone (turquoise, agate, jade, sodalite, lava stone)
  - Gold-filled and sterling silver findings, clasps, wire
  - Beading thread (Beadalon, FireLine)

Techniques: bead weaving, stringing, wire wrapping, resin casting
