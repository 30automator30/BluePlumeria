# Nalu: surfer on a breaking wave (on hold)

Two-part piece: a clear-resin wave and a coloured surfer that plugs into it.
Set aside before final files; no print files were built.

- `nalu.py`: wave (barrel curl at the left, peeling into a feathering shoulder;
  ragged lip, streaked face, sand base with shells and a plumeria; hollow and
  vented) and the surfer part (board with two keyed D-pegs, 0.15 mm clearance
  into sockets in the face). `python3 nalu.py --voxel 0.5 --preview`
- `surfer.py`, `human.py`: the surfer, posed from the MakeHuman base mesh (CC0,
  vendored in `mh/` with its licence) by aiming bones and IK, then smoothed.
- Previews: `preview_concept.png` (latest assembly), `preview_surfer.png`.

Status: the wave's spiral curl was liked. The human figure never satisfied
(proportions, chest, hips and shorts), across procedural, realistic, stylized
and in-between versions. To revive it: a professionally sculpted, commercially
licensed surfer STL fitted to the board and sockets, or a non-human rider.
