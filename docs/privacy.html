Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 November 2026

Blue Plumeria is a small handmade-jewelry studio. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit blue-plumeria.com or buy from us, why, who helps us handle it, and the choices you have. Questions: hello@blue-plumeria.com.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to sell and ship your order, answer your messages, and keep the site working.
  • We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use advertising trackers.
  • Google Analytics runs only if you choose Accept in the cookie banner. You can change that any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.
  • We never see or store your card number. Payments are handled by our checkout provider.

What we collect, and why

When you place an order

Your name, email address, shipping and billing address, the items you bought, and order totals. We use them to process payment, ship your order, send order emails, handle returns and questions, and keep the records that tax and accounting rules require. Checkout is run by Snipcart, and card payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go straight to them; we never receive them. A copy of each order (without card details) is stored in our order database.

When you use the contact form

Your name, email address, and message. We store the message so we can reply, and we email ourselves a notification so we see it quickly.

When you chat with "Ask the studio"

The chat is an AI assistant. To write a reply, what you type is sent to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI model. We don't save chat conversations. If you ask us to get in touch, or request a custom piece, the assistant saves your name, email, and request so we can follow up. Please don't share sensitive personal information in the chat.

Automatically, when you browse

  • Google Analytics (only if you accept): pages viewed, products looked at, items added to the cart, purchases, and general device and location information. It uses cookies. We use it only to understand which pieces people like and how the shop is used.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: counts page visits without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Your IP address, briefly, to stop abuse of the chat and contact form. These records are deleted within a few hours.
  • Fonts are currently loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser's IP address is sent to Google when a page loads. We plan to host the fonts ourselves.
  • Our website host and the services above keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

Cookies and similar storage

  • Needed for the shop to work: your cart and checkout (Snipcart), and remembering your cookie choice. These don't need your consent.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics cookies, set only after you choose Accept. If you later decline, we remove them.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a "no" to analytics automatically.

Who helps us

We share personal information only with the services that run the shop for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Snipcart (cart and checkout) and Stripe (card payments)
  • Supabase (our database: orders, contact messages, chat follow-up requests)
  • Anthropic (writes the chat assistant's replies)
  • Resend (sends email notifications to us)
  • Google (analytics, only if you accept; fonts)
  • Cloudflare (cookie-free visit counts) and GitHub (hosts the website)
  • Shipping carriers, who receive your name and address to deliver your order

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

How long we keep it

  • Orders: as long as we need them for accounting, tax, and returns.
  • Contact messages and chat follow-up requests: as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the conversation, then deleted on request.
  • Chat conversations: not stored by us.
  • IP addresses for abuse prevention: a few hours.
  • Google Analytics data: kept by Google according to the retention setting on our account.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (except records we're legally required to keep, such as tax records). Email hello@blue-plumeria.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. We'll respond within 45 days. We won't treat you differently for making a request. Depending on where you live, your state's privacy law may give you further rights, and we'll honour them.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards: encrypted connections (HTTPS), payment handled by specialist providers, and access to order and message data limited to the studio owner. No system is perfectly secure, so we'll tell you promptly if a problem affects your information.

Children

This shop isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
