The Atelier

Our Story

A working atelier

Blue Plumeria is a working atelier — a place where design ideas take shape through hands-on exploration of texture, color, and form. Every piece begins as an experiment and evolves into something meant to be worn and felt.

Our Studio Series represents this process: small-batch, limited editions crafted from natural stones, crystals, freshwater pearls, and copper wire. No two pieces are exactly alike. Each one carries its own character and energy.

The name "Blue Plumeria" draws from the plumeria flower — delicate, vibrant, and full of life. Blue represents calm, creativity, and depth. Together, they capture the spirit of a brand in motion: intentional artistry, always evolving.
